About Me

I am currently working as an Architectural Designer at Stratus (formally known as Zyscovich) and am on the path to becoming a licensed architect. I recently adopted a neighborhood tuxedo cat and named him Elmer! I enjoy hiking, traveling, running, and exploring new cuisines.

Ambitions

My goal is to become a licensed architect in Florida and develop skills in property development with a focus on custom homes.

Projects

3D Printing

I enjoy designing functional 3D prints that range from everyday household objects to more elaborate models used for architectural studies. In my professional work, I also produce models of completed projects and 3D print them for clients as a way to showcase the firm’s work and highlight the successful collaboration between the firm and its partners.

Bee Keeping and Removal

What began as a summer class at the University of Florida eventually grew into a side venture with my father, where we safely relocate feral bee colonies into managed hives to promote pollination and saving the bees!
